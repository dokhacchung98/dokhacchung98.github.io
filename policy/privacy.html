Universal Application Scope

This Privacy Policy applies universally to all current and future mobile games, applications, and interactive software developed, published, or operated by AlphaVN / Do Khac Chung on any distribution platform, including the Google Play Store and Apple App Store. By downloading, accessing, or playing any of our games, you consent to the practices described in this policy.

1 General Principles & Zero Personal Harvesting

Our core design philosophy is to deliver lightweight, engaging, and enjoyable gaming experiences without unnecessary data collection:

  • No Account Required: Our games can be fully played without registering a user profile, account, or submitting your name, email, or physical address.
  • No Sensitive Permissions: We do not request or access private mobile sensors such as GPS precise location, contact lists, microphone audio, camera streams, or personal gallery photos.
  • Encrypted in Transit: Any network communication performed by integrated third-party SDKs is strictly encrypted using modern HTTPS (TLS) protocols.

2 Information Collected Automatically

When you play our games, certain non-personally identifiable technical information is automatically processed by verified third-party mobile development SDKs:

  • Device Identifiers: Standard mobile advertising IDs (such as Google Advertising ID / AAID on Android, or Identifier for Advertisers / IDFA on iOS if authorized) and randomized device instance IDs.
  • Hardware & Software Data: Device model, manufacturer, operating system version, screen resolution, language settings, and general network carrier name.
  • Usage & Performance Diagnostics: Crash logs, framerate performance, application launch timestamps, and in-game error stack traces used exclusively to identify and repair bugs.

4 Children’s Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Protecting the privacy of children is of paramount importance to us:

  • Our games are created for general audiences (primarily age 13 and above) and do not knowingly collect personal data from children under the age of 13.
  • In compliance with the United States Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and international child privacy regulations, if we discover that personal information has been inadvertently gathered from a child under 13 without verifiable parental consent, we will take immediate steps to delete such information from our records.
  • Parents or legal guardians who have any concerns may contact us directly at ochung3@gmail.com for prompt assistance.

5 Global Privacy Rights (GDPR & CCPA/CPRA)

Depending on your location, you hold specific legal rights concerning your digital privacy:

  • European Economic Area (GDPR) / UK: You have the right to request access to, rectification of, or erasure of any data processed about you, as well as the right to restrict or object to automated data processing.
  • California Residents (CCPA/CPRA): You have the right to know what personal data categories are collected and request deletion. We do not sell your personal information for monetary value.
  • Right to Non-Discrimination: Exercising any privacy right will never degrade your gameplay experience or result in denied access to our applications.

6 How to Control & Reset Your Advertising ID

You hold direct control over mobile advertising identifiers at any time directly through your device settings:

  • On Android: Open Settings → Google → Ads → tap "Reset advertising ID" or "Delete advertising ID".
  • On iOS: Open Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking → toggle off tracking permissions or customize individual apps.

7 Data Retention & Deletion Requests

Because our games do not register accounts or maintain centralized user profiles on proprietary backend databases, we do not hold personal player databases.

Game state data (such as levels cleared, unlocked heroes, high scores, and audio settings) is stored locally on your own device and can be permanently wiped at any moment by clearing the application's storage in your device settings or uninstalling the app.

If you have questions about third-party diagnostics or wish to submit an inquiry regarding data associated with an advertising identifier, please email us with your request.
